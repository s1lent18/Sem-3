Functions:

    a function f from a to b, denoted f: a -> b assigns
    each element of A to exactly one element of B. Functions
    may also be called mapping or transformations

        A       B
        1       A
        2       B
        3       C
        4       D
        5       E

    if 1 from A is connected to B in B and 3 from A is also 
    connected to B in B then
    Image:
        B will be the image of 1 under f

    PreImage:
        {1,3} are the preimage of B under f

    Range:
        All the value of B that are mapped to the elements of A
        will be in the Range

    One-to-One & Onto-Functions:

        Injective Functions:(one-to-one)
            Every Value in the range corresponds to exactly one
            element in the domain

        Surjective Functions:(onto-one)
            Every Element in the codomain maps to atleast one element
            in the domain

        Bijective Functions:
            Functions that are both Injective and Surjective.

        Inverse Functions:
            Let f be a bijection from set A to B. The inverse of f, f^-1,
            is the function from B to A defined as f^-1(y) = x iff f(x) = y
