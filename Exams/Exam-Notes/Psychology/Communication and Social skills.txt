Communication and Social skills:

Communication:
    the process of passing information and understanding the same from
    one person to another through verbal and non verbal means

    Functions of communication:
        - informing
        - persuading
        - integrating
        - creating relationships
        - help in making selections
        - reducing misunderstanding
        - making decisions
    
    Types of communication
        Verbal communication
            - Oral communication
            - Written Communication
        
        Non-Verbal Communication

    Non-Verbal Communication:
        includes:
            - eye contact
            - facial expression
            - gestures
            - postures
    
    7 C's of Communication:
        - Completeness: The message must have all the information needed
        - Clarity: Stressing on a particular message or goal at a time
        - Conciseness: Non repetitive in nature and saves time
        - Concreteness: being particular and clear rather than fuzzy and general
        - Courtesy: positive message and focused on the audience
        - Consideration: no grammatical mistakes in the sentence
        - Correctness: All sentences must be connected to each other

    4 style's of Communication:
        - Passive: 
            - avoid expressing their opinions and feeling
            - don't responds to hurtful and anger-inducing situations
            - doesn't stand up for their rights
            - no eye contact and low pitch to voice
            - Lack of Confidence

            Impact on themselves:
                - feel anxious because they dont feel in control
                - feel depressed because they are stuck and hopeless

        - Passive-Aggresive:
            - Mumble to themselves rather than confronting
            - Deny there is a problem
            - Use Sarcasm
            - Use facial Expression that dont match their feelings

            Impact on themselves:
                - become isolated from those around them
                - remain stuck in a position of powerlessness
                - Discharge resentments while real issues are never
                  addressed

        - Aggresive:
            - Stands up for his rights but violates the rights of others
            - Creates Win/Lose Situations
            - People tend to avoid/hate them
            - Non-Verbal cues: pointing fingers etc
            - have low frustration tolerance
            - Act's Rudely

        - Assertive:
            - Creates Win/Win Situations
            - Balance Everyone's needs
            - Communicates respectfully
            - Haved Relaxed Body Posture
            - Stand up for his rights
            - Good eye contact

            Impact on themselves:
                Feel connected to others
                address real issues and problems
                creates a respectfull enviorment for others

Social Skills:
    are commonly defined as those specific behavioral strategies that allow
    one to:
        - Develop Friendships and support networks
        - Cope Effectively with the enviorment
